Privacy Policy

Arthsutra.AI — a product of Stark Industries
Effective date: 16 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Arthsutra.AI ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information, including how it uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to send business notifications on behalf of the businesses that operate the App.

1. Who We Are

Arthsutra.AI is a billing, inventory, and GST-compliance application used by small and medium businesses to manage invoices, parties (customers and suppliers), payments, and statutory filings. Arthsutra.AI is developed and published by Stark Industries.

2. Information We Collect

The App stores the following information, entered directly by the business using it:

  • Business/company details (name, GSTIN, address, bank details)
  • Customer and supplier details (name, phone number, WhatsApp number, GSTIN, address)
  • Invoices, payments, and inventory records created by the business

This data is stored locally on the business's own device/database. Arthsutra.AI does not operate a central server that collects this data from multiple businesses.

3. How We Use WhatsApp Business Messaging

Businesses using Arthsutra.AI may optionally enable WhatsApp notifications for their customers and/or suppliers. When enabled, and only when a party's WhatsApp number is provided, the App uses the WhatsApp Business Platform (operated by Meta) to send the following transactional notifications:

  • Invoice Created — sent when a new invoice is generated for that party
  • Payment Received — sent when a payment is recorded against an invoice
  • Payment Reminder — sent for outstanding/overdue invoices

To deliver these messages, the recipient's phone number and the message content (invoice number, amount, and date) are shared with Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform strictly for the purpose of message delivery. Meta's own WhatsApp Business Data Processing Terms govern how Meta processes this data on our behalf.

We do not use WhatsApp messaging for unsolicited marketing. All messages are transactional and directly related to a business transaction (invoice or payment) already initiated by the recipient's relationship with the business.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, except:

  • With Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform, solely to deliver the notifications described above
  • Where required by law or regulation (e.g., GST filings submitted directly by the business to government portals)

5. Data Retention & Control

Business data, including party phone numbers and message logs, is retained locally by the business for as long as they continue to use the App. The business administrator can:

  • Disable WhatsApp notifications at any time from the App's Settings
  • Remove or edit a party's WhatsApp number at any time, which stops future notifications to that number
  • Delete party records entirely from the App

6. Your Rights

If you are a customer or supplier who has received a WhatsApp message from a business using Arthsutra.AI and wish to stop receiving messages or have your number removed, please contact that business directly — they control the data entered into the App.
